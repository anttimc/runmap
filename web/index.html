    How to read environment scores

    All layers start Off. Score layers are transparent where the score is zero or data is unavailable; use Coverage to distinguish them. Mapping completeness is unverified: zero does not establish real-world absence. Traffic is an uncalibrated exposure proxy. Wetland presence does not establish path wetness.

    Colors show terrain slope, rather than steepness along a path.
    Green outlines mark paths with a flat stretch of at least 50 m. Display only.
    Colors show steepness along paths. Click a planned route to explore elevation.
    Color the map by Distance from start: shortest distance along paths, in 500 m bands. Extra travel (distance): distance beyond a straight line, in 200 m bands. Extra travel (%): extra distance divided by straight-line distance, with a 500 m minimum denominator to stabilize values near the start. For example, 1.5 km along paths versus 1 km directly is 50% extra travel. Highlights detours around water, railways and other obstacles. Distance colors scale to the largest extra distance in the explored area. Percentage colors are capped at the 99th percentile; higher percentages use the top color.
    The budget limits distance along paths in both color modes. The same start is used for run planning. Return distance is not included.
