About distance highlighting

Colors show 500 m bands of shortest legal distance along the path network. Filled areas between paths are approximate; they do not imply access across water or other barriers. Return distance is not included. Click a mapped path to inspect its exact network distance.

How loop width works

A soft preference to go around the midpoint, not a minimum clearance. Zero disables shape penalties while keeping route preferences. Each direction is planned separately; both may choose the same route when other preferences dominate.

Turning centre avoidance off keeps side and reuse preferences at nonzero widths.

About hill avoidance

Prefer gentler, longer climbs over short steep climbs. The running energy curve from Minetti et al. (2002) is adjusted to soften climbs below 5% and increasingly discourage steeper climbs. Only effort above flat running adds a hill penalty; flat terrain and moderate descents add none. Bridge, tunnel, and missing terrain estimates use a conservative fallback. Terrain estimates are independent of the map background.
