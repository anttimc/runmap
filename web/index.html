    Map layers
    Score layers show mapped context from 0 (clear) to 1 (strong), at 10 m resolution. Greenery appears when Environment is switched on. Transparent areas can mean zero or unavailable data; Coverage distinguishes them. Gray means prepared coverage; amber marks limited context. Traffic exposure is an uncalibrated proxy, and wetland presence does not establish path wetness. Mapping completeness is unverified.

    Color the map by Distance from start: shortest distance along paths, in 500 m bands. Extra travel (distance): distance beyond a straight line, in 200 m bands. Extra travel (%): extra distance divided by straight-line distance, with a 500 m minimum denominator to stabilize values near the start. For example, 1.5 km along paths versus 1 km directly is 50% extra travel. Highlights detours around water, railways and other obstacles. Distance colors scale to the largest extra distance in the explored area. Percentage colors are capped at the 99th percentile; higher percentages use the top color.

    About Suunto export

    Uses an unofficial Suunto API through your locally configured account. The Suunto app transfers selected routes to your watch during sync. Elevation represents smoothed ground height; turn instructions are not included.

    Reopening after a successful export starts a new export with an editable name. Pending exports keep their name locked for status recovery. Check your Suunto library if creation is unconfirmed, then choose Start new export to send another route.
